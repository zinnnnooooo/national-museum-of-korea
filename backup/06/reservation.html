Visitor Guidelines (관람 안내)

  • Opening Hours (관람 시간) Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun: 10:00 - 18:00
    Wed, Sat: 10:00 - 21:00 (Night Opening)
  • Admission Fee (관람료) Free Entry for Main Permanent Exhibition Galleries.
    Special exhibitions may require separate ticketing.
  • Reservation Terms (예약 유의사항) Please present your mobile confirmation or phone number at the entrance gate. Reservations can be modified up to 1 hour before entry.
  • Lockers & Facilities (편의 시설) Free luggage lockers, strollers, and wheelchairs are available near the main lobby.
