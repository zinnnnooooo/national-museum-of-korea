Opening Hours & Admission

  • Operating Hours (관람 시간)
    Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun: 10:00 - 18:00 (Entry until 17:30)
    Wed, Sat: 10:00 - 21:00 (Night Opening, Entry until 20:30)
    Closed on New Year's Day, Lunar New Year's Day, and Chuseok.
  • Admission Fee (관람료)
    Main Permanent Exhibition Galleries: Free Entry.
    Special exhibitions may require separate admission tickets.

Transportation & Location

  • Address (주소)
    137 Seobinggo-ro, Yongsan-gu, Seoul 04383, Republic of Korea
  • Subway (지하철)
    Line 4 / Gyeongui-Jungang Line - Ichon Station (이촌역) Exit 2 (Connected directly via Moving Walkway).
  • Bus (버스)
    Blue Bus 400, 502 / Green Bus 0411 (National Museum of Korea Stop).

National Museum Building

Set against Namsan Mountain and overlooking the Han River, the National Museum of Korea is one of the world's largest museum complexes, housing over 420,000 national treasures and historical artifacts.
