THREE KINGDOMS PERIOD · 7TH CENTURY

Pensive Bodhisattva

Gilt-bronze Pensive Bodhisattva (National Treasure No. 83)

A masterpiece of Korean Buddhist sculpture, capturing a timeless moment of quiet contemplation.

PERIOD Three Kingdoms Period
DATE Early 7th Century
MATERIAL Gilt-bronze
CATEGORY Buddhist Sculpture
COLLECTION National Museum of Korea
EXHIBITION ARCHIVE

A Moment of
Quiet Contemplation

This Gilt-bronze Pensive Bodhisattva represents the peak of religious expression and metalcraft in ancient East Asia.

The statue depicts the Bodhisattva in a pensive pose, with one leg crossed over the other and fingertips gently touching the cheek. This posture represents the prince Siddhartha meditating on the nature of human life and suffering before attaining enlightenment.

The gentle, serene smile on the face, the natural folds of the robe flowing down the pedestal, and the delicate crown on the head reflect a high degree of artistic sophistication, making it an iconic representation of Korean cultural heritage.
